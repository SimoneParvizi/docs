Blue Accademy's flashcard system gives you a structured way to build lasting knowledge of Kubernetes, Docker, and Pulumi. Cards are grouped into topic-focused decks, and a built-in spaced-repetition scheduler ensures you review each card at exactly the right moment — surfacing it just before you would forget it, and pushing the next review further out each time you recall it successfully.

## How it works

Decks organize cards by subject area. When you open a deck and start studying, the scheduler builds a queue of cards that are currently due — a mix of brand-new cards you have never seen, cards still working through their early learning steps, and older cards returning for a long-term review. You see the question side of a card first. When you are ready, you flip the card to reveal the answer. After reading the answer you rate how well you recalled it. The scheduler uses that rating to calculate when to show the card next: a poor rating brings it back very soon; a strong rating pushes it further out.

## Card states

Every card moves through four states as you study it.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | You have never reviewed this card. It enters the queue subject to your daily new-card limit. Cards in this state have a blue border during study. |
| **Learn** | The card is in its early step progression. It reappears in the same session or the next day, following your configured learning steps, until it graduates to Review. Cards in this state have a red border. |
| **Relearn** | The card was previously in Review but was rated Blackout. It works through the relearning steps before returning to the Review state. Cards in this state also have a red border. |
| **Review** | The card has graduated to long-term retention. The scheduler spaces reviews over days, weeks, or months based on your rating history. Cards in this state have a green border. |

## Rating scale

After you flip a card, four rating buttons appear. Each rating affects how soon the card returns.

| Rating | Value | What it means | Effect on interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blackout** | 0 | Complete blank — you could not recall anything | Card enters (or re-enters) relearning from the first step |
| **Hard** | 1 | Difficult recall — you remembered with significant effort | Interval is shortened compared to a Good rating |
| **Good** | 2 | Correct with effort — the expected result | Card advances to the next learning step, or the interval grows at the standard rate |
| **Easy** | 3 | Instant recall — no hesitation at all | Card jumps ahead to your configured easy review interval, skipping remaining learning steps |

The exact number of days until next review is shown in small text beneath each rating button, so you can see the consequence of your choice before you tap.

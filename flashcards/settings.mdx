Flashcard settings control how quickly new cards enter your study flow and how intervals evolve after each rating.

## How to open settings

Settings are managed from the flashcards UI and apply to the flashcard system as a whole.

## Settings reference

<ParamField body="newCardsPerDay" type="integer" default="2">
  Maximum number of new cards introduced per day.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="learningSteps" type="string" default="10m 1d 3d">
  Space-separated learning step durations for new cards.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="relearningSteps" type="string" default="10m">
  Space-separated relearning durations for cards that were forgotten from review.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="minimumInterval" type="integer (days)" default="1">
  Minimum allowed review interval in days.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="easyReviewInterval" type="integer (days)" default="9">
  Review interval used when a card is rated Easy from new or learning.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="normalReviewInterval" type="integer (days)" default="7">
  First review interval used when a card graduates through the normal learning path.
</ParamField>

## Learning steps format

Step-based settings use space-separated duration tokens:

| Suffix | Unit | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `m` | Minutes | `10m` — 10 minutes |
| `h` | Hours | `2h` — 2 hours |
| `d` | Days | `3d` — 3 days |

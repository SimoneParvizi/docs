Decks are the primary organizational unit in Blue Accademy's flashcard system. Each deck covers a specific topic within a learning track, and every deck has its own card queue, study stats, and settings. You can study the included pre-seeded decks right away, create your own decks, or import cards from Anki — all from the same Flashcards page.

## Creating a deck

Click the **folder+ icon** (🗁+) in the deck list header to open the New Deck panel. Fill in the following fields and click **Create Deck**:

- **Title** (required) — the name shown in the deck list.
- **Description** (optional) — a short summary of what the deck covers.
- **Track** — choose one of `kubernetes`, `docker`, `pulumi`, or `azure`. This determines which filter tab the deck appears under.

## Renaming a deck

Click the **settings icon** (⚙) on any deck row to open the Deck Settings panel. Update the name in the **Deck name** field, then click **Save Name**. The new name takes effect immediately in the deck list.

## Deleting a deck

Open the Deck Settings panel for the deck you want to remove (click the ⚙ icon on the deck row). Click **Delete Deck**, then confirm by clicking **Delete Permanently** in the confirmation prompt.

<Note>
  Deleting a deck is irreversible. All cards in the deck and their complete review progress history are permanently removed. There is no way to recover a deleted deck.
</Note>

## Track tags

Every deck has a color-coded track badge shown on its row in the deck list. To move a deck to a different track, click the track badge on that row. A small picker opens listing all four available tracks. Click the desired track to reassign the deck. Available tracks are:

- `kubernetes` — blue badge
- `docker` — cyan badge
- `pulumi` — violet badge
- `azure` — sky blue badge

## Filtering by track

Above the deck list, a row of filter tabs lets you narrow the view by track. Click **All**, **Kubernetes**, **Docker**, **Pulumi**, or **Azure** to show only decks in that track. The filter applies immediately without a page reload.

## Per-deck stats

Each deck row in the list shows three card counts at a glance:

| Column | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **New** | Blue | Cards you have never studied that are available today (respects your daily new-card limit) |
| **Learn** | Red | Learning or relearning cards with a step due now |
| **Due** | Green | Review cards whose scheduled review date has arrived |

Next to the deck title, a `seen/total` count (for example, `12/40`) shows how many cards in the deck you have reviewed at least once out of the deck's total card count.

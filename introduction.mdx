Blue Accademy is an open-source learning platform that helps you build lasting fluency in Kubernetes, Docker, Pulumi, and adjacent infrastructure workflows. Instead of passively reading documentation, you study with a spaced-repetition flashcard system that surfaces the right cards at the right time — so the concepts you find hardest get reviewed more often, and the ones you've mastered stay out of your way.

## Key features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Spaced-repetition scheduling" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    A built-in SM-2-style scheduler automatically queues cards based on your recall rating, so you spend more time on what you don't yet know.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pre-seeded decks" icon="layer-group">
    Blue Accademy ships with starter decks for Kubernetes, Docker, and Pulumi so you can begin studying immediately after first boot — no card creation required.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Anki import support" icon="file-import">
    Bring your existing knowledge base with you. Import `.apkg` Anki decks directly into Blue Accademy and continue studying with the same scheduler.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom card creation" icon="pen-to-square">
    Add your own flashcards to any deck at any time, tailoring your study material to the specific tools, versions, or workflows you care about.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Configurable scheduler settings" icon="sliders">
    Tune the scheduler to match your study cadence — adjust intervals, ease factors, and review thresholds to fit your schedule.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deck management" icon="folder-open">
    Create, edit, and delete decks freely. Organise your cards into focused topic areas or broad reference collections — the structure is up to you.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What's available now

<Note>
  The flashcard system is stable and ready to use today. You can create and manage decks, study with spaced repetition, add custom cards, import Anki decks, and tune the scheduler settings from day one.

  The following features are still under active development and are not yet available in this release:
  - **Guided terminal exercises** — currently being migrated to the new Python/FastAPI backend
  - **CKAD-style simulation** — not yet supported; PRs for this area will not be accepted at this time
  - **Chat and hosted sandbox orchestration** — not yet supported in this release
</Note>

## What you can do

Pick up Blue Accademy today and start putting these capabilities to work:

- **Create and manage decks** — build focused collections around any Kubernetes resource type, Docker workflow, or Pulumi concept
- **Study with spaced repetition** — let the scheduler decide what to review so every session is as efficient as possible
- **Add custom cards** — extend the pre-seeded content with your own questions and answers
- **Import Anki decks** — migrate your existing `.apkg` files and keep all your cards in one place
- **Tune scheduler settings** — configure review intervals and ease factors to match the way you learn

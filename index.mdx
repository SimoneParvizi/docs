Blue Accademy helps you build and retain knowledge of Kubernetes, Docker, and Pulumi through spaced-repetition flashcards. Start with pre-seeded decks that cover hundreds of core concepts, create your own cards, or import existing Anki collections — then let the scheduler surface the right cards at the right time.

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the stack">
    Start the backend and frontend with Docker Compose. The database is seeded automatically on first boot with decks for Kubernetes, Docker, and Pulumi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Navigate to `http://localhost:5173` and go to the **Flashcards** page to see all available decks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Study a deck">
    Click any deck to enter study mode. Flip cards by clicking, then rate your recall — **Blackout**, **Hard**, **Good**, or **Easy** — and the scheduler will queue the card again at the right time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your collection">
    Add your own cards, import an existing Anki export, or create a brand-new deck around any topic you want to master.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The flashcard system is the stable, production-ready feature of Blue Accademy. Terminal exercises, CKAD simulation, and chat-based sandbox features are still in development and not yet available.
</Note>

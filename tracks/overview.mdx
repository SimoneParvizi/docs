Learning tracks are curated collections of flashcard decks that guide you through a technology from first principles to advanced operations. Instead of studying random cards in isolation, you follow a deliberate sequence — each deck builds on the last so that concepts click together rather than pile up. Blue Accademy ships three seeded tracks — **Kubernetes**, **Docker**, and **Pulumi** — and a fourth track tag, **Azure**, ready for your own custom decks. Each seeded track contains multiple pre-built decks, each focused on a distinct aspect of the technology, so you always know exactly where you are in your learning journey.

## How Tracks Work

Each track groups its decks on the **Flashcards** page. Use the track filter tabs at the top of the deck list to narrow the view to only the decks that belong to a given track. Clicking **Kubernetes**, **Docker**, **Pulumi**, or **Azure** instantly filters out all other decks so you can focus on one technology at a time.

<Note>
  You can assign any custom deck you create to a track using the **track badge picker** in the deck list. Select your deck's overflow menu, open the badge picker, and choose the track that best fits your content.
</Note>

## Suggested Approach

Work through each track's decks in the order listed on the track page. The seed decks are sequenced so that foundational concepts come first, giving later decks the context they need to make sense. Once you feel confident with the seed content, create your own decks and assign them to the relevant track to keep everything organized in one place.
